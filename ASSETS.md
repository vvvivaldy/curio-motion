# Artwork and media provenance

The root MIT licence covers original source code. Reference artwork, generated references, recorded audio and dependencies have their own provenance and terms. No code licence grants rights to unrelated artwork or changes Remotion's licence.

## References and drawings

- Pink monkey: the whole AI-generated design reference was created with the built-in image-generation tool. [Prompt](reference/IMAGEGEN_PROMPT.txt) and [input manifest](authoring-input.json) disclose its origin. It is authoring material, not a runtime texture; no exclusive copyright guarantee is made.
- Amber robot: [design.svg](reference/amber-robot/design.svg) is an original whole vector study authored locally before animation. The robot, watering can and scene are drawn in code.
- Black cat: the character design follows a drawn mascot in an owner-supplied photograph. The photograph remains private, authoring-only and excluded from Git/build/package. Code licensing and reference-artwork rights are separate.
- Cat story: the charcoal cat, room, chair, clock and landscape picture are code-authored vector forms. Five whole style references remain private; their pixels and cut-up parts are not runtime materials.
- Boar beta studies: an owner-supplied whole screen recording is pinned in [its manifest](reference/serious-boar/authoring-input.json) and excluded from distribution. No redistribution permission for that video or its audio is inferred. The boar, packages and setting are locally authored vector drawings.
- Raccoon and infographic: whole authoring references remain local-only and are excluded. The courier is code-authored geometry. Chart values 12/18/30 are explicitly synthetic educational data, not delivery statistics.

Input/authoring manifests retain whole-input identities and privacy declarations. Private inputs are not needed to run the included scenes.

## Project films and documentation artwork

Included MP4s are project-created renderer output. The current cat GIF is a derivative of its official MP4; the header and social preview are documentation artwork. Scene renderers never use those movies, GIFs or reference pixels as character textures.

[EXAMPLES](docs/EXAMPLES.md) separates selected current films from beta studies. Superseded exports and review sheets are available in the unchanged v1.0.0 Git snapshot. Their historical inspection records apply to those versions.

MorningDelivery combines the existing courier and educational chart with new vector titles. The owner accepted its 36-second film. It retains the courier audio credits below.

[November Cancelled](docs/NOVEMBER_CANCELLED.md) is the English version of a CurioWorks cartoon. It retains the original artwork, animation, music and sound effects. English speech was generated with Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0 model); only the finished audio is distributed.

## Recorded footsteps and wooden chair

The 59 footstep OGG sources from seven materials were compiled by congusbongus from named Freesound authors under the distributed pack's **CC BY 3.0**. The seven notices, individual authors, source URLs and original-page licence facts remain in [credits](public/audio/sfx/CREDITS.md) and [catalog](public/audio/sfx/catalog.json). This pack is not MIT or CC0. Selected tile WAVs use uniform 0.5 headroom gain and short fades; preparation commands and hashes are retained.

The active chair recording is unkleceeg's Freesound276473, CC0 1.0. Official HQ MP3 previews are preserved as downloaded originals, not native lossless WAV masters. A second cut and a Trashcan_Studios CC BY 4.0 alternative keep their own notices. [Chair credits](public/audio/sfx/chair/CREDITS.md) and `assets/sfx/chair` retain the source, licence evidence and cut recipe.

The rejected procedural HumanChair V1 contains original seeded effects; [provenance](public/audio/human-chair/provenance.json) identifies their generator and parameters. It is a beta experiment, replaced in current examples by recorded V2.

## Cat story recordings

Ten story cuts come from six official Freesound HQ MP3 previews, plus the existing CC0 chair cut. “Cat Hiss” by cdonahueucsd ([Freesound620962](https://freesound.org/people/cdonahueucsd/sounds/620962/)) is **CC BY 4.0**. The clock, wood-drop, barefoot-tile, scrape and fabric recordings retain their named creators and CC0 notices in [story credits](public/audio/sfx/story/CREDITS.md) and [catalog](public/audio/sfx/story/catalog.json).

Tile and fabric recordings are disclosed foley for paws/grooming, not recordings of actual cat paws/fur. Cut/gain/fade processing and source/derivative hashes are retained; no music or spoken voice is added.

## Courier recordings

The expansion includes 16 WAV variants from 13 source recordings by 12 accounts. CC0 notices, primary pages, official HQ MP3 previews, cuts/fades/gains and hashes remain in `assets/sfx/courier`; runtime WAVs, [credits](public/audio/sfx/courier/CREDITS.md) and catalog remain in `public/audio/sfx/courier`.

Banknote/book-page and leather-jacket sounds are disclosed foley substitutions for the envelope and bag. Downloaded previews are not lossless masters. Provenance is not a guarantee of acoustic fit or absence of background speech. Keep required attribution with derived films; inspect and listen to the actual result.
