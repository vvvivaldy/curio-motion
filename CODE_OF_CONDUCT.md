# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and visible results, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

When a repository exists, maintainers may edit/remove abusive content or restrict participation. Use the repository's available private reporting channel for sensitive concerns; if none exists, ask a maintainer for one without posting private details publicly. No email address, moderation team or response SLA is invented for this unpublished beta.
