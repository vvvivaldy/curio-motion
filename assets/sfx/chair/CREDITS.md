# Recorded chair candidates: credits and reuse

These files derive from real recordings described by their authors on the linked primary Freesound pages. The preserved downloads are the official public HQ MP3 previews; native WAV masters were not downloaded because they require login. The resulting PCM WAV files do not recover lossless master quality. No imported game assets, AI generation or synthesized creak is used.
